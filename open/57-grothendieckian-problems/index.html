Abstract, as published

We state fifty-seven open problems drawn from the seven works of the Grothendieckian Programme (34 volumes, 26,021 pages, released openly in October 2026 as first drafts that have not been peer-reviewed). Thirty-five come from the two works on the architecture of a reasoning system and twenty-two from the five works that each take a named conjecture or programme as research subject; no problem is one of those conjectures. Each problem is stated in the words of its source, located by work, chapter and page, given the status the source assigns it, and paired with a criterion that decides when it is solved, a note on existing work and the date of a literature check. The problems are of three kinds: twenty-five mathematical problems, nineteen machine problems with success criteria fixed in advance, and thirteen formalization problems for Lean 4 and Mathlib. Every quotation from the sources has been checked against the published PDFs, and each problem has been checked against the literature as of 28 October 2026; a machine-readable version accompanies the paper.

Use of AI tools

The main ideas and the architecture of the Grothendieckian Programme are the author's and come from the author's research at WiseMotif.ai. In the seven works, AI tools assisted with drafting and with part of the literature collation; all of the text was reviewed by the author. Lean verification of the seven works is incomplete. Because of their size, the works are first drafts (Research Architecture Edition 1) and have not been peer-reviewed; they need review by experts and further work on the programme. They are released openly for that purpose, in the hope of further contributions and of more recursive mathematical discovery in AI for mathematics. For the present paper, statements were extracted from the published works by script and checked against the PDFs; AI tools assisted with drafting and with literature collation; the author selected the theses and problems and reviewed every statement.
