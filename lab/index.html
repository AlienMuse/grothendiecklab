Grothendieck Lab

The official site of Grothendieck Lab and of the Grothendieckian Programme.

Grothendieck Lab is the laboratory of WiseMotif.ai in which the Grothendieckian Programme is developed.

The Programme is rooted in Grothendieck's EGA and SGA and in his way with a hard problem: to build the setting in which the problem becomes natural, and to pose the next question there. It develops new algorithms for machine reasoning about mathematics, drawing on modern AI theory and on reinforcement learning, with a reward verified by descent rather than predicted.

Its platform is recursive mathematical discovery: recursive self-improvement (RSI), held to the standard of proof. Each generation learns from the evidence of the last, its failures included, while every record stays fixed and no candidate certifies itself. Whether the recursion succeeds is, in the Programme's own words, an empirical question; every claim carries its status.

The method

Recursive mathematical discovery is recursive self-improvement, held to the standard of proof.

What improves, recursively, is the mathematical world and the machine that holds it. No candidate certifies itself, and what fails is kept as evidence for the next generation.

The resulting picture differs from common accounts of recursive self-improvement. Recursion here means immutable succession under stable constraints. Generation n + 1 uses evidence from generation n, including failures and causal analyses, while the record and target of n remain fixed.

— Series 01, Vol. VI, p. 13063

The purpose of Volume VI is to show that meaningful self-improvement and strict epistemic restraint can reinforce one another.

— Series 01, Vol. VI, p. 13075

The success of recursive mathematical discovery, the formalizability of important research judgments, and the reliability of governance remain empirical and institutional questions.
