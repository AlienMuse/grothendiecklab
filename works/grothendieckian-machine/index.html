Abstract, as published

GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine is a six-volume program in mathematics, formal reasoning, system architecture, and self-evolving mathematical artificial intelligence. Its organizing conviction is that major advances frequently arise not from manipulating isolated formulas more aggressively, but from constructing an ambient world in which the objects, transports, invariants, and local-to-global laws of a problem become visible.

The work has two inseparable obligations. The first is mathematical: to explain schemes, topoi, descent, cohomology, motives, higher structures, modern arithmetic geometry, and their successors with exact hypotheses and source-aware proof lineage. The second is architectural: to express mathematical worlds as typed, versioned, provenance-bearing artifacts that can be searched, compared, formalized, computed with, and evolved without allowing statistical confidence to masquerade as proof.

The phrase Grothendieckian Machine names a research architecture, not a completed universal solver. Its purpose is to make mathematical world construction, descent, comparison, obstruction, and reconstruction explicit enough to support rigorous human and machine collaboration.
